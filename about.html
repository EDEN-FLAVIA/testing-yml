OUR STORY

What is LearnSphere?

LearnSphere brings useful online learning resources together in one simple and accessible place.

◈

A simpler way to discover learning

LearnSphere is a frontend-only online learning platform created to help learners discover useful free courses across multiple technology domains.

Instead of searching through countless learning resources, users can explore a curated course catalogue, understand what each course offers, and directly access the original learning platform.

OUR MISSION
